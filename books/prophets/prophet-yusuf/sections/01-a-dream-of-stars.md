# Section 1 — A Dream of Stars

Have you ever had a dream that made you want to tell someone about it?

The Qur'an tells us about a dream Prophet Yusuf (peace be upon him) shared with his father, Prophet Ya'qub (peace be upon him).

Yusuf told his father that he had seen eleven stars, the sun, and the moon in his dream. He had seen them bowing low before him. It was an unusual dream, and Yusuf chose to tell his father about it.

His father listened and gave him careful advice. Ya'qub told Yusuf not to tell his brothers about the dream, because they might make a plan against him. He also reminded Yusuf that Shaytan (Satan) is a clear enemy to people.

Ya'qub did not stop there. He told Yusuf that Allah would choose him, teach him how to understand the meaning of events, and complete His favour upon him and the family of Ya'qub. He connected this favour with what Allah had already given to Yusuf's forefathers, Ibrahim and Ishaq (peace be upon them).

The Qur'an does not tell us the colour of the stars, where Yusuf was standing, or what the room looked like when he spoke to his father. We do not need to add those details. The important parts are clear: Yusuf shared his dream, and his father answered with guidance.

A dream can leave us with questions. When we have something important to talk about, it can help to speak with a trusted grown-up who listens and gives careful advice. We can listen, think about what we are told, and ask questions when we do not understand.

This is the beginning of Yusuf's story. The dream will be remembered again later in the Qur'an, after many events have taken place. For now, we begin with a son sharing something important with his father—and a father offering guidance.

**For curious listeners (ages 5–6):** The Qur'an returns to Yusuf's dream near the end of the story, in Surah Yusuf 12:100. The names of the eleven stars are not given in the Qur'an, so we will not add names to the story.

**The lesson:** We can listen carefully to wise advice from a trusted grown-up.

**Talk together:** Who is a trusted grown-up you can talk to when you have an important question?

**Try it today:** Share one question or something you have been thinking about with a trusted grown-up.

**Illustration note:** A quiet night sky with eleven stars, the sun, and the moon shown as symbolic objects. Do not show any people, silhouettes, prophets, or Allah.
