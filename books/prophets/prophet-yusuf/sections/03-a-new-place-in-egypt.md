# Section 3 — A New Place in Egypt

Yusuf was far from his father and his home. The Qur'an tells us that he had been put into a well by his brothers. What would happen next?

A group of travellers came by. They sent someone to draw water from the well. When the traveller lowered his bucket and pulled it up, he found Yusuf. The traveller was glad to find a boy there and called out with good news.

The travellers took Yusuf with them. The Qur'an explains that they treated him as something to sell. They later sold him for only a small price. Yusuf had not chosen to leave his family, and what had happened to him was not fair.

The travellers went on to Egypt. There, a man from Egypt bought Yusuf. The man spoke to his wife and told her to make Yusuf's stay comfortable. He said that Yusuf might be useful to them, or they might take him as a son.

Notice how different this place was from Yusuf's home. He was now in a household in Egypt, among people he had not grown up with. The Qur'an does not tell us the colour of the house, what the rooms looked like, or what Yusuf ate, so we do not need to make those details up. The important part is that Yusuf's life had changed greatly.

Allah tells us that He established Yusuf in the land and would teach him the interpretation of events. Later, when Yusuf had grown to maturity, Allah gave him wisdom and knowledge.

Yusuf's difficult journey did not mean that Allah had forgotten him. The Qur'an shows that Allah's plan was continuing, even though Yusuf's circumstances had changed in ways he could not have chosen or understood at the time.

This does not mean that every difficult change will quickly become easy, or that we always know why something happens. Sometimes children have to move to a new home, start at a new school, or spend time away from familiar people. Those changes can feel hard. A child can ask a trusted grown-up for help, and a Muslim can ask Allah for strength and guidance.

Yusuf's story reminds us that a person's situation can change, but we should not decide that their story is finished just because one part has become difficult. The Qur'an does not tell us everything Yusuf experienced during this time. It tells us what we need to know for the next part of his story: he was in Egypt, and Allah was giving him wisdom and knowledge.

As Yusuf grew, new challenges would come. He would need to keep choosing what was right, even when it was not easy.

**A word to know:** *Wisdom* means understanding how to make good and thoughtful choices.

**For curious listeners (ages 5–6):** Surah Yusuf 12:21–22 connects Yusuf's new life in Egypt with the wisdom and knowledge Allah later gave him.

**The lesson:** A difficult change does not mean that we have been forgotten.

**Talk together:** What helps you feel safe when something in your life changes?

**Try it today:** If something feels new or difficult, talk with a trusted grown-up and ask for the help you need.
