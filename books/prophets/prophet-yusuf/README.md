# Prophet Yusuf: A Story of Patience and Forgiveness

- **Book ID:** `prophet-yusuf`
- **Status:** In progress — Section 1 draft awaiting parent review
- **Audience:** Children aged 3–6, read aloud by a caregiver
- **Language:** English; explain occasional unfamiliar words in brackets
- **Format:** Approximately 4–5 minutes per section
- **Illustrations:** Never depict Prophet Yusuf, any other prophet, or Allah. Use setting-only, object-based, landscape, architecture, and symbolic imagery.

## Parent-facing summary

This book follows the account of Prophet Yusuf in Qur'an 12:4–101: his dream, his brothers' wrongdoing, his time in Egypt, his refusal to do wrong, his imprisonment, the interpretation of dreams, his public responsibility during years of hardship, and his eventual reunion with his family. The Qur'an is the primary narrative source. The outline deliberately omits extra names, dates, dream-star names, and dramatic details not established by the primary passage.

## Reading approach

The core account should remain understandable to ages 3–4. Difficult events must be stated gently and honestly, without graphic detail or invented dialogue. Optional notes for ages 5–6 may add source-grounded context but must not be necessary to follow the story.

## Scope and review note

This is an age-adapted retelling plan, not a substitute for Qur'anic study or religious rulings. The parent should review the treatment of family conflict, the household incident in 12:23–35, imprisonment, and the later brothers' encounter before any section is drafted.
