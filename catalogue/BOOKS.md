This is an idea list, not a claim that all stories have been researched or approved. Every book must go through the source-backed skeleton process before any child-facing section is written. Prioritise a coherent, manageable story rather than promising a full biography when reliable sources are limited.

## Status definitions

- **Idea — not researched:** candidate only; no source-backed outline exists yet.
- **Skeleton — needs parent review:** research register and outline exist; no story sections have been generated.
- **In progress:** one or more section drafts exist.
- **Needs source review:** unresolved evidence issue requires attention.
- **Reviewed:** parent has reviewed the relevant content. Do not use this status for the whole book until its scope is clear.

## Selection guidance

Choose the next book based on:
1. Strength and clarity of primary sources.
2. Suitability for ages 3–6.
3. A focused narrative arc that can be told in short sections.
4. Variety of values across the library.
5. Avoiding repetition across books.

The first suggested book is `prophet-yusuf`, because Surah Yusuf provides a substantial, connected Qur'anic account. Its outline still needs proper research, age adaptation, and parent review before story sections are written.
