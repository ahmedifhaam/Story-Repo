# Section 2 — A Painful Plan

In the last part of the story, Prophet Yusuf (peace be upon him) told his father about his unusual dream. His father, Prophet Ya'qub (peace be upon him), gave him careful advice and reminded him of Allah's favour.

But Yusuf's brothers were troubled by jealousy. They thought their father loved Yusuf and his younger brother more than he loved them. Instead of talking honestly about their feelings, some of the brothers began to make a harmful plan.

They spoke about sending Yusuf far away. One of them suggested that they should put him into a well, where travellers might find him. They thought that if Yusuf were gone, their father would pay more attention to them.

The brothers asked Ya'qub to let Yusuf go out with them. They said they would look after him. Ya'qub felt worried about letting Yusuf go, but the brothers continued to ask. At last, they took Yusuf with them.

Then they carried out their plan. They put Yusuf into the well. The Qur'an does not tell us every detail of what happened there, so we will not add frightening pictures or imagined words. It does tell us that Allah gave Yusuf reassurance that one day he would remind his brothers of what they had done, even when they did not recognise him.

Later, the brothers returned to their father at night, crying. They told Ya'qub that a wolf had eaten Yusuf while they were away. They brought Yusuf's shirt with false blood on it, hoping their story would be believed.

Ya'qub did not accept their account as the truth. He recognised that they had done something wrong. But he did not answer by doing something wrong himself. He chose **patient endurance**—patience while facing something painful—and asked Allah for help with what they were saying.

Think about the brothers' choice. Feeling jealous can happen to people, but jealousy does not give anyone permission to hurt another person. The brothers could have spoken honestly about how they felt. Instead, they chose to harm Yusuf and deceive their father.

Ya'qub's response shows another way to face a difficult moment. He was deeply concerned for Yusuf, but he turned to patience and sought Allah's help. Patience does not mean pretending that wrong actions are right. It means trying to respond in a way that is pleasing to Allah, even when we are upset.

Yusuf's story had become difficult, and he was far from his family. But the story was not over.

**A word to know:** *Jealousy* is the unhappy feeling we may have when we think someone else is getting something we want.

**For curious listeners (ages 5–6):** In Surah Yusuf 12:15, Allah reassures Yusuf that he will one day tell his brothers about what they did. The verse does not say that Yusuf knew all the details of his future.

**The lesson:** Jealous feelings do not make it right to hurt someone.

**Talk together:** What could you do if you felt jealous or left out?

**Try it today:** If you feel upset, tell a trusted grown-up how you feel instead of being unkind to someone.
